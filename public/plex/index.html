Pick Your Platform

Plex can be enjoyed on a variety of platforms from your smartwatch to a 146-inch TV. Select a platform below to get started!

Mobile

Visit https://plex.tv/web or download the app off of your app store and press sign up. Once you have made your account, press the button below and type in the email that you used to create your account. Visting the sorting tab at the top once you recieve your link.

Note: The iOS and Android Plex apps restrict playback to 1 minute if you don't pay the one-time $5. However, the web version is free and the app can still be used to cast to a TV. Leaving a small donation includes full access to these apps :) (learn more)

TV

Open the Plex app on your TV/Console and, using a mobile/computer device, create an account at https://plex.tv/web. Then, link your account using the instructions displayed on screen. Once you have made your account and linked it to your TV, press the button below and type in the email that you used to create your account. Visting the sorting tab at the top once you recieve your link.
