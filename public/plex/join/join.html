Frequently Asked Questions

Since Plex charges a one time $5 fee to unlock the Android and iOS Apps and allow playback for longer than one minute, I haven't gotten around to writing instructions. If you are willing to pay, just download the app, login with your account, and set up as usual.

Plex can run on almost anything that connects to the internet. That is the beauty of the platform. Plex runs free on Browsers (Desktop + Laptop), Streaming Devices (Smart TVs, Amazon Fire TV, Android TV, Apple TV, Chromecast, Roku, etc), and Gaming Consoles. The mobile Plex app (Android/iOS) requires a $5 one time fee to unlock, but it is well worth it in my opinion.

Plex itself is actually just a piece of software that takes your movie/show files (mp4, mkv, avi, etc) and projects them in a Netflix/Disney+/Hulu style interface that can be accessed from any device. There is actually no catch. The reason I run my server for free is because it's fun and I also use it myself.

The best thing that you can do to help is request movies and shows. Also, donations are accepted and encouraged but not forced ;)
